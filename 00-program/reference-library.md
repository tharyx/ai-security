# Reference Library: Your Documents

An index of the PDFs in the local `Documents/` folder, mapped to the modules they support.
**The PDFs are not committed** (`.gitignore` excludes `Documents/`). Many are copyrighted, and together they are about 400 MB. Keep them on your machine; this page tells you what to read when.

The lessons were aligned to these documents in their own words, and each module README ends with a "Further reading from your library" list.
