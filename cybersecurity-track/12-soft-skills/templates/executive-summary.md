# Executive summary template

> One page. Lead with the answer. A manager should be able to decide after reading the first five lines. Replace the italic text.

**To:** _name / role_  **From:** _you_  **Date:** _YYYY-MM-DD_  **Topic:** _short title_

## Bottom line (2 sentences)

_What happened or what is the risk, and what you recommend. Example: "Our customer portal had a weakness that let a stranger read 400 customer records for 3 hours. It is fixed. We recommend telling legal today, so they can decide about notification."_

## Why it matters to the business

- **Impact in business words:** _money, customers, law, deadlines, reputation_
- **Size:** _numbers: how many users, how much money, how long_
- **Likelihood:** _already happened / likely / possible / unlikely, and why_

---

### Quick checks before sending

- [ ] First 5 lines make sense to someone outside IT.
- [ ] No unexplained acronyms. Where needed: "MFA (a second proof of identity)".
- [ ] Numbers, not "many" or "serious".
- [ ] Clear owner and date for each action.
- [ ] Facts are separate from guesses.
- [ ] No sensitive details that this reader does not need.
