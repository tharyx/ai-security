## A simple study plan (weeks 4–7)

1. Read one lesson, do the exercises and lab, answer the interview questions without looking.
2. At the end of each week, do 30–50 practice questions from a reputable source and write down every wrong answer.
3. In week 7, take full-length practice exams. Study the domains where you score under 80%.
4. Book the exam only when you pass practice tests comfortably. Read the official page for current price and format (performance-based questions are part of it).

Tip: the library's old Security+ SY0-601 notes are useful for concepts. Their numbering is from the older exam, so use them as a reading aid, not as the objective list.
